# AICTE | IBM SkillsBuild Internship: Data Analytics with AI
## Deliverable 2: Supermarket Sales Analysis & Business Intelligence

**Organization:** BharatCares (SMEC Trust) in collaboration with IBM SkillsBuild & AICTE  
**Domain:** Retail Analytics & Business Intelligence  
**Duration:** 6 Weeks (August – September 2026)  

### Project Overview
This project performs end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on transactional records across three supermarket branches (Yangon, Mandalay, Naypyitaw) to evaluate branch revenue, product line profitability, and customer purchasing behavior.

### Key Metrics
- **Total Records:** 1,000 transactions
- **Total Gross Revenue:** $312,052.12
- **Average Basket Size:** $312.05
- **Gross Profit Margin:** $14,859.94
- **Average Customer Rating:** 6.98 / 10

### Key Insights & Recommendations
1. **Product Line Performance:** Sports & Travel and Food & Beverages drive top gross revenue. Health & Beauty provides cross-selling bundle opportunities.
2. **Customer Demographics:** Loyalty members show highest adoption of digital E-wallet transactions.
3. **Branch Operations:** Recommendation to standardize cashier checkout training at Branch B to match Branch A/C rating standards (>7.5/10).
